Fingerspell L, X and G

Make each letter with your right hand. Your camera checks your handshape and shows you what to change, as you go.

  1. Hold your phone in your left hand at arm's length, or prop it up.
  2. Fit your right hand into the glove on screen. The blue lines are your hand.
  3. When the glove turns green, hold still until the ring around the letter fills.
